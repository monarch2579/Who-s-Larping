How to Play

Overview: A social deduction game played in-person! All players receive a secret context except the Imposter(s). The group must deduce who is the imposter through clues while the imposter attempts to blend in.

🎮 Mode A: Secret Word

All Real Players are shown a secret word (e.g. "Volcano"). The Imposter is shown only the Category (e.g. "Nature"). Each player takes turns saying a clue out loud that hints at the secret word. The Imposter must bluff and say a clue that fits the category.

🎮 Mode B: Secret Question

All Real Players are shown the same personal question. The Imposter is shown a nearby question and must answer convincingly to fit the group's conversation.

💬 Discussion & Voting

After all players give clues, the group debates out loud. Analyze the clues and try to identify the Imposter! Then, everyone points at the person they suspect on a count of three. The player with the most points is eliminated. Report the result to the app.

🏆 Winning & Guessing

If a Real Player is eliminated, the Imposter survives and wins the round. If the Imposter is eliminated, they get a dramatic comeback chance: the app presents them with 4 choices to guess the secret word or question. If they guess correctly, they steal the victory! Otherwise, the Real Players win.
